🛒 The Legend of the Wobbly Cart

You have been assigned Cart #4092 at the MegaMart 24/7 parking lot. Three of its wheels are somewhat cooperative. The fourth caster wheel has achieved centrifugal sentience and rotates at 4,000 RPM, pulling you violently to the starboard side at all times.

🎯 How to Survive:

  • Steering: Counter-steer constantly to fight the demonic rightward drift.
  • Pigeons: Pigeons love roaming for french fries. Charge through them to scatter them into the sky!
  • Speed Bumps: Hit them at speed to jump, but beware: your groceries might bounce right out!
  • Oil Slicks: Dark iridescent oil puddles eliminate all wheel friction. Prepare for 720° spins.
  • Parked Cars: Don't smash too hard or you'll trip the anti-theft alarms.
  • Kicking the Wheel: Press K to deliver a calibrated steel-toed kick. It might briefly silence the squeak... or cause an RPM overload!
